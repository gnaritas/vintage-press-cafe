Privacy

Short version: this site collects only what you choose to send us.

What we collect

The contact form asks for your name, and optionally your phone and email, plus your message. We use it for one thing only: answering you.

No trackers

This site sets no cookies of its own, runs no analytics, and loads no third-party scripts. Pages that show a location map load Google Maps inside a frame, and Google may set its own cookies there — see Google's privacy policy.

Where your message goes

Form messages are delivered straight to the café's inbox, and only the café team reads them. Ask and we'll delete yours: hello@vintagepresscafe.com.
