Our Story

A print shop, reborn.

Vintage Press Café opened in November 2026 at 256 Salmon Brook Street — a historic Granby building that spent its first life as a working print shop.

Why “Vintage Press”

The name came from the walls. Salmon Brook Street runs through Granby Center Historic District — the district named for the town's old village center — and this building made its living in printing long before it made ours. A press makes careful impressions, one at a time. So do we, just with coffee instead of ink.

The menu has a memory too

Every drink is named after an influential woman from history — our nod to the women who set things in motion. The building remembers presses; the menu remembers the women who made history move.
