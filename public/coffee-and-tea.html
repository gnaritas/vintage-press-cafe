Coffee & Tea

Specialty coffee & loose-leaf tea

We brew specialty coffee, and we take tea seriously too — leaves, not bags, steeped to order.

The coffee

Specialty coffee, from open to close: hot, iced, or whatever the bar tells you to try. Served every day between 7 a.m. and 2 p.m.

The tea

Loose-leaf tea all day long — measured, steeped, and poured while you settle in.

Named for history-makers

Every drink on our menu — coffee and tea alike — carries the name of an influential woman from history. Ask about the names while you wait; they're good conversation.
